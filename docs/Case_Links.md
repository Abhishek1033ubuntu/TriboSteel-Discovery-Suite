### 1. International Standards & Industrial Failure Atlases

* **ISO 15243 Standard (*Rolling bearings — Damage and failures*):**
The global standard defining the classification of rolling element bearing damage, distinguishing surface-initiated vs. subsurface-initiated fatigue, false brinelling, micro-spalling, and fretting corrosion.

[ONYX Insight — Fretting Corrosion Bearing Failures](https://onyxinsight.com/resources-support/failure-atlas/fretting-corrosion-bearing-failure/)
*Focus:* Micro-motion fretting corrosion, false brinelling, and raceway degradation in wind turbine main-shaft and gearbox bearings.
* **ASM International Failure Analysis Case Studies:**
[ASM International / ResearchGate — Fretting and False Brinelling Case Analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Severe-damage-from-fretting-false-brinelling-on-the-surface-of-a-shaft-that-served-as_fig1_257746266)
*Focus:* Diagnostic case studies of micro-sliding, contact fatigue, and surface damage on bearing raceways and journal fits.

### 3. Summary of Core Failure Mechanics Documented

| Mechanism | Physical Process | Industrial Manifestation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Micro-Motion Slip ($1\text{--}50\,\mu\text{m}$)** | Oscillatory micro-sliding breaks protective passive oxide film at contact interface. | Localized micro-fretting on raceways and bore fits. |
| **Third-Body Abrasive Wear** | Wear debris rapidly oxidizes into hard hematite particles ($\alpha\text{-Fe}_2\text{O}_3$, $1000\text{--}1200\text{ HV}$). | Abrasive micro-pitting and progressive surface spalling. |
| **Fretting Fatigue Initiation** | Subsurface shear stress peaks ($\tau_{\text{max}}$) combined with tensile stress concentrations at contact edges. | Rapid micro-crack initiation and premature fatigue spalling. |
| **Surface Mitigation Strategy** | Surface texturing, solid-lubricant retention dimples, or thermochemical surface compression ($\sigma_{\text{res}} \le -700\text{ MPa}$). | Halts subterranean crack propagation and eliminates fretting rust. |
